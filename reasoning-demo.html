01 / FIRST ATTEMPTGCSE Foundation-style · Algebra

First, solve the equation.

3(x + 4) = 27

Find x. Then select the method that best explains how you arrived at your result.

Which method describes your reasoning?

WHAT WE NOTICE

Answers are evidence.
Methods explain them.

A correct answer can hide an incorrect method. A sensible method can still lead to an arithmetic slip. Both deserve different feedback.

Try the question before revealing the explanation.No timer. No account. No pressure.
