## GCSE Mathematics — Foundation | Grove Weekly | Reverse percentages | 5 marks

**Question.** A coat costs £63 after a 30% reduction. Find its original price. A shop then increases that original price by 10%. Find the new price.

**Worked solution.** The sale price is 70% of the original: `0.70 × original = 63`. Thus `original = 63 ÷ 0.70 = £90`. A 10% increase on the **original** price gives `£90 × 1.10 = £99`.

**Proposed criterion marks (5).** M1 recognise 70% multiplier; A1 original £90; M1 increase **original** price by 10%; A1 £99; B1 label both monetary answers correctly. An independent examiner must check whether the final B1 is defensible for the specific board; if not, revise the mark allocation before moderation.

**Arithmetic verification.** `90 × 0.70 = 63`, `90 × 1.10 = 99`.

## IGCSE Mathematics — Core | Grove Daily | Ratio | 5 marks

**Question.** A fruit drink is made by mixing concentrate and water in the ratio 2:7. A café makes 4.5 litres of the drink. Find the volumes of concentrate and water in millilitres.

**Worked solution.** `2 + 7 = 9` parts. `4.5 L = 4500 mL`. One part is `4500 ÷ 9 = 500 mL`. Concentrate is `2 × 500 = 1000 mL`; water is `7 × 500 = 3500 mL`.

**Proposed criterion marks (5).** M1 identify 9 parts; B1 convert 4.5 L to 4500 mL; M1 find 500 mL per part; A1 concentrate 1000 mL; A1 water 3500 mL.

**Arithmetic verification.** `1000 + 3500 = 4500` and `1000:3500 = 2:7`.

## IB Mathematics AA SL | Grove Exam | Quadratic modelling | 6 marks

**Question.** The height of a ball above the ground in metres after `t` seconds is `h(t) = −5t² + 20t + 1`, where `t ≥ 0`. Find the maximum height and when it occurs. Find the positive time when the ball reaches the ground, correct to 3 significant figures.

**Worked solution.** Complete the square: `h(t) = −5(t − 2)² + 21`. The maximum height is `21 m` at `t = 2 s`. Ground impact occurs when `−5t² + 20t + 1 = 0`. By the quadratic formula, `t = (20 ± √420)/10`. The positive root is `4.049390... s`, giving **4.05 s** to 3 s.f. The negative root is outside the model domain.

**Proposed criterion marks (6).** M1 complete square or vertex method; A1 `t = 2`; A1 `21 m`; M1 set `h = 0` and solve quadratically; A1 positive-root expression; A1 `4.05 s` (3 s.f.).

**Arithmetic verification.** `h(2) = −20 + 40 + 1 = 21`; substituting `(20 + √420)/10` into `h` yields zero up to floating-point precision.
