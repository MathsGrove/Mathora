Why we ask for working

Every step tells a story.

A correct result matters, but the reasoning behind it shows what to practise next. Your teacher will eventually distinguish method, accuracy and independent credit.

  1. Set up the methodShow which mathematical idea you chose.
  2. Follow each calculationMake units, substitutions and signs easy to inspect.
  3. Check and explainTry a reverse calculation or reasonableness check.

When operational, human moderation and secure release must precede any real learner or family feedback.
