## Grove Daily — short fluency and targeted correction

### D-GF · GCSE Foundation · fractions · 2 marks
**Question:** A class collected 28 reusable notebooks. Three quarters were plain. How many were plain?

**Working:** $28\div4=7$ and $7\times3=21$.
**Answer:** 21 notebooks.  
**Indicative marks:** M1 for quartering 28 or multiplying by 3/4; A1 for 21.  
**Feedback focus:** A fraction of a quantity is not the same as subtracting three quarters.

### D-GH · GCSE Higher · algebra · 3 marks
**Question:** Solve $x^2-7x+12=0$.

**Working:** $(x-3)(x-4)=0$, hence $x=3$ or $x=4$.
**Indicative marks:** M1 correct factorisation; A1 each distinct root.  
**Feedback focus:** Check the sum and product of the factor constants.

### D-IC · IGCSE Core · time · 2 marks
**Question:** A coach leaves at 09:35 and travels for 1 hour 48 minutes. At what time does it arrive?

**Working:** 09:35 + 1 hour = 10:35; + 48 minutes = 11:23.  
**Answer:** 11:23.  
**Indicative marks:** M1 correct addition of the hours and minutes; A1 arrival time.  
**Feedback focus:** Carry minutes across 60, not across 100.

### D-IE · IGCSE Extended · surds · 3 marks
**Question:** Simplify $\dfrac{2\sqrt{18}-\sqrt{8}}{\sqrt{2}}$.

**Working:** $\sqrt{18}=3\sqrt2,\ \sqrt8=2\sqrt2$; numerator $6\sqrt2-2\sqrt2=4\sqrt2$; quotient $4$.
**Indicative marks:** M1 correct square-factor decomposition; M1 collect like surds; A1 answer 4.  
**Feedback focus:** Simplify radicals before combining terms.

### D-AS · AS pure · differentiation · 3 marks
**Question:** Given $f(x)=3x^3-4x^2+5$, calculate $f'(2)$.

**Working:** $f'(x)=9x^2-8x$. Thus $f'(2)=9(4)-8(2)=20$.
**Indicative marks:** M1 differentiate both non-constant terms; M1 substitute 2 into derivative; A1 20.  
**Feedback focus:** Differentiate first, then substitute.

### D-AL · A Level pure · integration · 3 marks
**Question:** Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^1 \frac{2x}{x^2+1}\,dx$.

**Working:** Put $u=x^2+1$, so $du=2x\,dx$. The limits become $u=1,2$. Integral $=[\ln u]_1^2=\ln2$.
**Indicative marks:** M1 substitution; M1 use correct limits/antiderivative; A1 $\ln2$.
**Feedback focus:** Transform limits when substituting.

## Grove Weekly — structured multi-step reasoning

### W-GF · GCSE Foundation · direct proportion · 3 marks
**Question:** A bakery uses 300 g flour for 8 muffins. Assuming exact direct proportion, how much flour is needed for 20 of the same muffins?

**Working:** Scale factor $20/8=2.5$; $300\times2.5=750$ g.
**Indicative marks:** M1 identify scale factor or unit rate; M1 apply it to 300 g; A1 750 g.  
**Feedback focus:** Explain why the ratio is direct.

### W-GH · GCSE Higher · algebra and geometry · 4 marks
**Question:** A rectangle has perimeter 34 cm and area 60 cm². Find its two side lengths.

**Working:** Let sides be $x$ and $17-x$. $x(17-x)=60$, giving $x^2-17x+60=0=(x-5)(x-12)$. Side lengths 5 cm and 12 cm.
**Indicative marks:** M1 semi-perimeter relationship; M1 area equation; M1 solve quadratic; A1 both sides and units.  
**Feedback focus:** A perimeter is twice the sum of adjacent sides.

### W-IC · IGCSE Core · map scale and speed · 4 marks
**Question:** On a map with scale 1:25,000, two places are 6.4 cm apart. A person walks the corresponding actual distance at a constant 4.8 km/h. Find the distance in km and walking time in minutes.

**Working:** $6.4\times25{,}000=160{,}000$ cm $=1.6$ km. Time $=1.6/4.8=1/3$ hour $=20$ minutes.
**Indicative marks:** M1 apply scale; A1 1.6 km; M1 distance ÷ speed; A1 20 minutes.  
**Feedback focus:** Keep the distance and speed units consistent.

### W-IE · IGCSE Extended · quadratic sequences · 4 marks
**Question:** A quadratic sequence begins $5,12,23,\ldots$ and has $u_n=an^2+bn+c$. Find $u_n$, then find $u_{10}$.

**Working:** First differences 7, 11; second difference 4, so $a=2$. Using $u_1=5$ and $u_2=12$ gives $b=1,c=2$. Thus $u_n=2n^2+n+2$ and $u_{10}=212$.
**Indicative marks:** M1 infer (a=2); M1 obtain (b,c); A1 nth term; A1 212.  
**Feedback focus:** Second differences determine the quadratic coefficient.

### W-AS · AS pure · binomial expansion · 4 marks
**Question:** Find the first three terms in ascending powers of $x$ of $(1+2x)^4$. Use them to approximate $(1.1)^4$.

**Working:** $1+4(2x)+6(2x)^2+\cdots=1+8x+24x^2+\cdots$. Take $x=0.05$: $1+0.4+0.06=1.46$.
**Indicative marks:** B1 constant term; B1 linear term; B1 quadratic term; A1 approximation 1.46.  
**Editorial note:** The exact value is 1.4641. The question asks for the *truncated approximation*, not the exact value.

### W-AL · A Level pure · stationary points · 5 marks
**Question:** For $y=x^3-3x^2+2$, find and classify all stationary points.

**Working:** $dy/dx=3x^2-6x=3x(x-2)$; thus $x=0,2$. Corresponding points $(0,2)$, $(2,-2)$. As $d^2y/dx^2=6x-6$, the first is a local maximum and the second a local minimum.
**Indicative marks:** M1 first derivative; M1 solve derivative=0; A1 both coordinates; M1 second derivative or gradient-sign test; A1 correct classification.  
**Feedback focus:** Solving (dy/dx=0) locates, but does not classify.

## Grove Exam-preparation — longer open tasks, NOT unseen timed mocks

### E-GF · GCSE Foundation · arithmetic / percentages · 4 marks
**Question:** A group buys 3 adult tickets at £8 each and 4 child tickets at £5 each. A 10% discount applies to the whole purchase. Find the final payment.

**Working:** Before discount $3(8)+4(5)=44$ pounds. Discount $0.10(44)=£4.40$; final $£44-£4.40=£39.60$.
**Indicative marks:** M1 adult total; M1 child total and combined £44; M1 discount; A1 £39.60.  
**Feedback focus:** Calculate percentage discount on the combined pre-discount price.

### E-GH · GCSE Higher · right-triangle trigonometry · 5 marks
**Question:** A right-angled triangle has perpendicular legs 9 cm and 12 cm. Let $\theta$ be the acute angle adjacent to the 12 cm leg. Find the hypotenuse, $\sin\theta$, and $\theta$ in degrees to 1 decimal place. **Not to scale; no diagram supplied.**

**Working:** Hypotenuse $\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=15$ cm. $\sin\theta=9/15=3/5$. $\tan\theta=9/12=0.75$, so $\theta\approx36.9^\circ$.
**Indicative marks:** M1 Pythagoras; A1 15 cm; M1 correct sin/tan ratios; A1 $3/5$; A1 36.9°.
**Feedback focus:** Choose opposite/adjacent relative to the specified angle.

### E-IC · IGCSE Core · probability · 3 marks
**Question:** A bag contains 4 red, 3 blue and 3 green counters. Two are taken at random **without replacement**. Find the probability both are red.

**Working:** $P(\text{red then red})=(4/10)(3/9)=12/90=2/15$.
**Indicative marks:** M1 first red probability 4/10; M1 conditional second red probability 3/9; A1 2/15.  
**Feedback focus:** The second denominator is 9 because no counter is replaced.

### E-IE · IGCSE Extended · simultaneous equations · 5 marks
**Question:** The curves $y=x^2-4x+1$ and $y=2x-3$ meet at two points. Find the exact coordinates of both.

**Working:** Equating gives $x^2-6x+4=0$; $x=(6\pm\sqrt{36-16})/2=3\pm\sqrt5$. Then $y=2x-3=3\pm2\sqrt5$, with matching signs.
**Answer:** $(3+\sqrt5,3+2\sqrt5)$ and $(3-\sqrt5,3-2\sqrt5)$.
**Indicative marks:** M1 equate expressions; M1 quadratic formula; A1 x values; M1 substitute in line; A1 coordinate pairs.  
**Feedback focus:** Keep the matching sign in each ordered pair.

### E-AS · AS pure/mechanics candidate · motion · 5 marks
**Question:** A particle moves along a straight line with displacement $s(t)=t^3-6t^2+9t$ metres for $t\ge0$, with $t$ measured in seconds. Find the two positive times at which it is instantaneously at rest, and the distance travelled *between those times*.

**Working:** $v=ds/dt=3t^2-12t+9=3(t-1)(t-3)$. Rest at $t=1,3$. $s(1)=4$ m and $s(3)=0$ m. Since $v<0$ throughout $1<t<3$, movement is in one direction and distance is $4$ m.
**Indicative marks:** M1 differentiate; A1 both times; M1 calculate both positions; B1 show velocity does not switch sign within the interval; A1 4 m.
**Feedback focus:** Distance and displacement differ if direction changes.

### E-AL · A Level pure · differential equations · 5 marks
**Question:** Solve $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=2xy$ subject to $y(0)=3$. Give $y(1)$ exactly.

**Working:** $(1/y)\,dy=2x\,dx$. Integrate: $\ln|y|=x^2+C$, hence $y=Ke^{x^2}$. $y(0)=3$ gives $K=3$, so $y=3e^{x^2}$ and $y(1)=3e$.
**Indicative marks:** M1 separate variables; M1 integrate; A1 exponential general solution; A1 initial condition; A1 $3e$.
**Feedback focus:** Apply the initial condition after integrating.
